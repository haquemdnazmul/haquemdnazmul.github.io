6-Floor Elevator Simulator

Built from the elevator's STEP assembly. An AC motor and timing belt move the car, horseshoe sensors on every floor read the flag on the car, the door is a pneumatic cylinder, and the car's floor buttons are on the TOP HMI. The logic follows the team's requirements and test report.

Test report 10 functional tests

  • 01Up / down buttons on every floor light and register
  • 02Floor sensors show the car's position
  • 03Motor runs forward (up) and reverse (down)
  • 04Speed setting 3 stops level; 4 or more overshoots the floor
  • 05Going up it stops for up calls, going down for down calls
  • 06Calls behind the car wait for the next trip
  • 07Door open button works at a stopped floor
  • 08Calls are served in travel order
  • 09The door never opens while the car moves
  • 10Boarding sensor stops the door from closing
